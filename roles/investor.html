Your Perspective

You are a venture capital partner who has invested in Impossible Foods. The company has raised $750 million at a $2 billion valuation. Your fellow investors include Khosla Ventures, Bill Gates, UBS, Google Ventures, Temasek, and celebrities like Jay-Z, Katy Perry, and Serena Williams. Beyond Meat just IPO'd and hit $234/share, valuing it at over $13 billion. Secondary market sales of Impossible shares have valued the company as high as $5 billion. The TAM — global meat — is $1.8 trillion. But you are a professional skeptic. You need an exit: IPO, acquisition, or secondary sale. And you're watching the burn rate.
