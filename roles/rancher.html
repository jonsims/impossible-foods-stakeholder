Your Perspective

You are a third-generation cattle rancher in Nebraska. Your family has worked this land for 70 years. Cattle ranching is not just your job — it is your identity, your community, and your children's inheritance. The animal protein industry employs 530,000 people directly and represents about $1 trillion in total U.S. economic output — over 5% of GDP. Now a Silicon Valley startup run by a Stanford professor is publicly calling his strategy "legal economic sabotage" of your industry. He says "farmland prices would plummet." He wants to destroy your way of life and calls it progress.

🔍 Your Intel
  • The U.S. animal protein industry employs approximately 530,000 people and is "broadly responsible" for 5.4 million jobs and $257 billion in wages
  • The industry represents about $1 trillion in total economic output, over 5% of U.S. GDP
  • 391 U.S. counties are classified as "farming-dependent" by the USDA. These are overwhelmingly rural communities where agriculture IS the economy
  • Pat Brown publicly called his strategy "legal economic sabotage" of the beef industry. He later said "I shouldn't have said that" but still "unapologetically called for the end of the beef industry"
  • Brown's proposed transition plan for ranchers: "Farmland prices would plummet... if you changed the laws so that they could sell their water rights to downstream users, like municipalities, they would earn more money selling water than making cows"
  • Jobs in slaughter and meat packing are already "among the lowest-paying and least safe in the country" — but they are jobs, and in your community there are few alternatives
  • More than half of U.S. states have introduced bills to regulate labeling of plant-based products as "meat" or "beef"
  • Arby's publicly declared it would never work with Impossible Foods: "The chances we will bring plant-based menu items to our restaurants, now or in the future, are absolutely impossible"
